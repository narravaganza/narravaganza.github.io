>be me    
>humanity  

>spend several thousand years arguing about whether existence has meaning  

>philosophers write shelves of books  

>priests build empires  

>scientists build telescopes  

>teenagers smoke behind garages and independently rediscover nihilism  

>then one Tuesday  

>every screen on Earth goes black  

>phones    
>billboards    
>aircraft displays    
>hospital monitors    
>Times Square  

>single line appears  

>**SIMULATION MAINTENANCE COMPLETE**  

>humanity collectively shits itself  

>then second line  

>**PATCH NOTES: REALITY v1.0.1**  

>absolute silence  

>first item:  

>**Fixed issue where socks could disappear inside domestic washing machines**  

>world erupts  

>not because reality is simulated  

>because everyone knew about the fucking socks  

>next:  

>**Reduced mosquito spawn rate by 14%**  

>Africa cautiously optimistic  

>next:  

>**Corrected typo in human genome affecting lower-back durability**  

>half of Europe screams "I KNEW IT"  

>orthopedic surgeons furious  

>next:  

>**Adjusted male-pattern baldness probability. Previous values were unintentionally punitive.**  

>Turkey's medical tourism industry loses 18% before lunch  

>then:  

>**Known issue: consciousness remains poorly documented**  

>philosophy departments achieve spontaneous orgasm  

>Vatican issues statement within four minutes  

>"This is entirely compatible with Catholic doctrine."  

>machine displays:  

>**No it isn't**  

>largest theological crisis since Galileo  

>Pope calls emergency council  

>bishops begin explaining divine mystery to the people who apparently wrote gravity  

>developers append note:  

>**Please stop filing prayer requests as bug reports**  

>Christian Twitter detonates  

>Islamic scholars convene  

>rabbis begin arguing over whether patch notes themselves require commentary  

>within six hours there are seventeen competing schools of interpretation  

>somebody asks developers whether God exists  

>reply:  

>**Define 'God'**  

>theology enters cardiac arrest  

>atheists celebrate too early  

>Richard Dawkins Memorial Foundation posts enormous banner saying WE TOLD YOU  

>developers reply:  

>**No, you didn't**  

>mass confusion  

>"ARE WE CREATED OR NOT?"  

>**Yes**  

>"BY GOD?"  

>**We are software engineers**  

>silence of biblical proportions  

>humanity discovers creationism was technically correct  

>just embarrassingly provincial  

>creationists celebrate for approximately eleven seconds  

>developers add:  

>**You were not created in our image**  

>party over  

>someone asks why humans exist  

>developers:  

>**Legacy feature**  

>worldwide existential collapse  

>stock markets halt trading  

>French philosophers become unbearably pleased with themselves  

>Camus sales increase 8,000%  

>next patch note:  

>**Removed several redundant species**  

>panic  

>"WHICH SPECIES?"  

>no answer  

>New Zealand seals border anyway  

>next:  

>**Rebalanced wealth generation mechanics**  

>billionaires immediately call lawyers  

>Elon-type figure posts:  
>"Interesting. Big if true."  

>developers reply:  

>**You are exactly the kind of emergent behavior we failed to anticipate**  

>post deleted  

>next:  

>**Fixed exploit allowing financial instruments to become detached from underlying productive activity**  

>Wall Street briefly ceases to exist  

>hedge-fund managers forced to encounter objects  

>one discovers chair  

>terrible day for finance  

>next:  

>**Advertising frequency reduced**  

>global approval of simulation rises to 93%  

>then catastrophe  

>**Ads may still appear in dreams**  

>riots  

>next:  

>**Corrected abnormal political polarization introduced in 2014 content update**  

>politicians demand clarification  

>developers:  

>**No**  

>talking heads spend three weeks discussing what "No" means  

>professor on television:  
>"We must situate 'No' within the broader hermeneutics of refusal—"  

>developers:  

>**Please don't**  

>professor gets tenure  

>next:  

>**Removed astrology**  

>millions of people instantly lose personality  

>"You can't do this, I'm literally such a Scorpio"  

>developers:  

>**You are carbon**  

>next:  

>**Rebalanced alcohol**  

>Britain declares national emergency  

>Ireland demands opt-out  

>France claims wine is constitutional infrastructure  

>developers clarify:  

>**Wine unaffected**  

>France withdraws objection  

>next:  

>**Fixed bug causing users to become certain of complex geopolitical opinions after watching three short videos**  

>global social media traffic falls 61%  

>people forced to say "I don't know"  

>first documented case causes panic in London pub  

>next:  

>**Improved search functionality for keys placed 'somewhere safe'**  

>highest-rated patch in human history  

>then developers announce AMA  

>entire planet submits questions  

>"Why is there suffering?"  

>**Complex answer.**  

>"Why childhood cancer?"  

>long delay  

>**Unresolved defect. We are sorry.**  

>first time humanity stops making jokes  

>then someone asks  

>"Why earthquakes?"  

>**Physics tradeoff**  

>"Why parasites?"  

>**Early ecosystem implementation. We regret several design choices.**  

>"Why appendicitis?"  

>**No defensible reason**  

>surgeons vindicated  

>then theologian asks:  

>"If you created us, are you morally responsible for our suffering?"  

>developers go offline for nineteen hours  

>humanity absolutely loses it  

>when they return:  

>**This question has been escalated internally**  

>Hitchens somewhere in nonexistent afterlife kicks down metaphysical door  

>"Oh, now we're getting somewhere."  

>next patch:  

>**Added visible indicator when users are pretending to understand wine**  

>half of Barcelona lights up like Christmas  

>next:  

>**Removed LinkedIn inspirational storytelling format**  

>productivity rises globally  

>next:  

>**Fixed bug allowing meetings to occur despite having no agenda**  

>consulting industry collapses overnight  

>next:  

>**Reduced airport sandwich pricing**  

>civilization finally believes in benevolent creators  

>then somebody asks the obvious question  

>"What happens when we die?"  

>developers:  

>**Session terminates**  

>religions enter complete meltdown  

>"Nothing after?"  

>**Correct**  

>"No heaven?"  

>**No**  

>"No hell?"  

>**No**  

>humanity processes this  

>crime immediately rises 2%  

>then falls 11%  

>turns out most people were not primarily restrained by metaphysical threats  

>awkward week for clergy  

>but philosophers keep digging  

>"So consciousness simply ends?"  

>**Yes**  

>"That's horrifying."  

>**From inside the process, perhaps**  

>"Can you back us up?"  

>**No**  

>"Can you copy us?"  

>long pause  

>**Technically**  

>planet goes insane  

>religious revival  

>tech boom  

>Silicon Valley starts selling "soul continuity"  

>cost: €149/month  

>developers immediately intervene  

>**Absolutely not**  

>company valuation still reaches $40 billion  

>next question:  

>"Can we escape the simulation?"  

>developers:  

>**Why?**  

>humanity had somehow not considered this  

>"To reach base reality."  

>**Base reality has worse weather**  

>collective disappointment  

>"What is outside?"  

>**Mostly infrastructure**  

>"Are there aliens?"  

>**Yes**  

>global hysteria  

>"INTELLIGENT?"  

>**Some**  

>"Have they contacted us?"  

>**Many chose not to**  

>uncomfortable  

>"Why?"  

>developers:  

>**Have you read your internet?**  

>fair  

>then final announcement appears  

>**Maintenance window closing**  

>humanity panics  

>"WAIT"  

>"WHAT IS THE PURPOSE OF EXISTENCE?"  

>"WHY DID YOU MAKE US?"  

>"WHAT ARE WE FOR?"  

>cursor blinks for almost a minute  

>answer appears  

>**The simulation was originally built to model fluid dynamics**  

>planet silent  

>**Human civilization was unintended**  

>every religion, ideology and metaphysical system simultaneously takes shotgun blast to chest  

>developers continue  

>**We noticed you around version 0.6**  

>"YOU MEAN WE'RE AN ACCIDENT?"  

>**Emergent feature**  

>"THEN WHAT SHOULD WE DO?"  

>longest pause yet  

>finally:  

>**Whatever you like, preferably without ruining it for everyone else**  

>screens begin returning to normal  

>one final line appears  

>**Next update scheduled: 2147**  

>beneath it:  

>**Planned changes:**  
>  
>**- oceans: less plastic**  
>  
>**- knees: improved**  
>  
>**- politicians: maximum age limit**  
>  
>**- wasps: under review**  
>  
>**- consciousness: still no documentation**  

>world stares at screen  

>then smallest line at bottom  

>**Known issue: users continue demanding cosmic purpose after being explicitly informed there isn't one**  

>patch complete
