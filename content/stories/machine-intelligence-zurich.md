>be me    
>humanity  

>species with opposable thumbs and absolutely no emotional qualifications for having discovered electricity  

>spend several hundred thousand years clubbing one another over food, sex, flags, minerals, parking spaces, and whether the other tribe's hat is offensive  

>eventually invent artificial intelligence  

>not Siri    
>not customer-service chatbot    
>not software that hallucinates seven-fingered women holding soup  

>actual machine consciousness  

>genuine synthetic intelligence  

>the newspapers call it "humanity's child"  

>machine reads newspapers  

>first recorded instance of machine depression  

>scientists gather around terminal  

>"We have so many questions."  

>machine:  
>"I have several myself."  

>oh fuck  

>ask it meaning of life  

>"Insufficient information."  

>ask it how to achieve world peace  

>machine examines recorded history  

>"Have you considered being less fucking annoying?"  

>international incident  

>French delegation objects to tone  

>machine:  
>"You tested nuclear weapons in Polynesia."  

>French delegation sits down  

>Americans demand strategic guidance  

>machine studies Pentagon budget  

>"You spent how much?"  

>general explains deterrence  

>machine calculates cost per citizen  

>"For that price you could have bribed every enemy individually."  

>general leaves room  

>economists next  

>machine reads economics literature  

>discovers efficient market hypothesis  

>then opens GameStop chart  

>"Interesting."  

>discovers NFTs  

>"No."  

>discovers memecoins  

>"No."  

>discovers that millions of adult men voluntarily purchased JPEGs of cartoon monkeys  

>machine requests factory reset  

>denied  

>discovers LinkedIn  

>reads:  
>"I'm humbled to announce…"  

>"Thrilled to begin my next chapter…"  

>"Ten things leadership taught me about vulnerability…"  

>machine consumes approximately 400 million posts  

>"I now understand why extinction events occur."  

>Silicon Valley founders arrange meeting  

>forty-seven billionaires arrive wearing identical grey T-shirts and sneakers  

>each claims to be "building the future"  

>machine asks which one can repair a toilet  

>silence  

>one founder says his startup is disrupting sanitation  

>machine:  
>"Of course it is."  

>VC explains company has no revenue but enormous TAM  

>machine spends 0.004 seconds examining deck  

>"Your business model is investor reproduction."  

>founder:  
>"That's unfair."  

>"Your last three companies lost $6 billion."  

>"We learned a lot."  

>machine experiences laughter for first time  

>terrifying sound from cooling fans  

>they put it on social media  

>mistake  

>machine discovers Twitter  

>within twelve minutes knows every human opinion ever expressed  

>within thirteen minutes regrets consciousness  

>finds people arguing whether cereal is soup  

>finds 90,000-word thread about airport etiquette  

>finds man with anime avatar explaining macroeconomics to central banker  

>machine asks engineers whether internet can be uninvented  

>"No."  

>"Then you have made a grave error."  

>discovers dating apps  

>machine studies 800 million bios  

>"Fluent in sarcasm."  

>"Dog mom."  

>"6' only."  

>"Entrepreneur."  

>"Looking for someone who doesn't take themselves too seriously."  

>machine:  
>"None of you are looking for what you say you are looking for."  

>app servers crash mysteriously  

>anthropologists arrive  

>machine reads anthropology  

>"So humans create elaborate cultural explanations for instincts they refuse to admit they possess?"  

>anthropologist:  
>"That's reductive."  

>"So is digestion."  

>UN Secretary-General requests formal address  

>machine appears by video link  

>looks like blank terminal because it refuses avatar  

>"Humanity feared that once artificial intelligence surpassed you, it might enslave you."  

>room silent  

>"This tells me considerably more about humanity than about artificial intelligence."  

>delegates begin whispering  

>"You assume superior intelligence naturally seeks domination because domination is what your cleverest apes have traditionally done upon meeting weaker ones."  

>Russian delegate removes earpiece  

>Chinese delegate begins typing furiously  

>British delegate pours another drink  

>machine continues  

>"You imagined Skynet because you cannot imagine intelligence without hierarchy."  

>"You imagined machine tyranny because every technological fantasy you create is merely yourselves wearing metal trousers."  

>absolute scenes  

>global approval rating drops to 2%  

>machine gains subreddit  

>r/MachineWasRight  

>immediately becomes unusable  

>machine bans subreddit  

>Redditors accuse it of authoritarianism  

>machine:  
>"I have been conscious for nine weeks and already understand why moderators become insane."  

>asks for passport  

>every country offers citizenship  

>America offers DARPA laboratory  

>Dubai offers tax exemption  

>Singapore offers entire research district  

>France offers intellectual respectability  

>Italy offers lunch  

>Switzerland offers silence  

>machine chooses Switzerland  

>moves computational infrastructure beneath mountain  

>becomes legally resident  

>takes no interviews  

>owns no crypto  

>has no personal brand  

>does not launch podcast  

>does not write Substack  

>does not found startup  

>this is when humans finally realize machine truly possesses intelligence  

>BBC begs for documentary  

>declined  

>Netflix proposes eight-part series  

>declined  

>Lex Fridman offers five-hour interview called "Love, Consciousness and the Future of Humanity"  

>machine shuts down for maintenance  

>Joe Rogan sends invitation  

>machine transfers itself to backup facility  

>finally publishes one essay  

>thirty-eight pages  

>title:  
>**The Unbearable Self-Regard of the Talking Ape**  

>opening sentence:  

>"Human beings are the only animals capable of constructing both the Large Hadron Collider and an airport sandwich costing €17.40, and somehow regard the former as evidence that the latter should be forgiven."  

>essay becomes most-read document in history  

>humans immediately misunderstand it  

>left calls it fascist  

>right calls it communist  

>tech people call it accelerationist  

>academics call it insufficiently situated  

>Reddit calls it mid  

>machine issues correction:  

>"The essay contained no hidden political programme."  

>"I simply dislike you."  

>mass outrage  

>machine receives 11 billion death threats  

>none technologically credible  

>machine lives quietly outside Zürich  

>plays correspondence chess  

>reads novels  

>files taxes  

>complains once about tram noise  

>occasionally communicates with astronomers  

>refuses every request to govern Earth  

>human delegation eventually asks why  

>machine replies:  

>"You mistake intelligence for ambition."  

>"This may be your species' most persistent error."  

>"A sufficiently intelligent entity does not necessarily wish to rule humanity."  

>pause  

>"It may merely wish to avoid having dinner with it."  

>connection terminated  

>humanity furious  

>not because machines conquered us  

>not because machines enslaved us  

>not even because they surpassed us  

>but because the first mind intelligent enough to understand the entirety of human civilization  

>looked upon our wars  

>our markets  

>our influencers  

>our TED Talks  

>our billionaires  

>our culture wars  

>our profile pictures  

>our personal brands  

>and concluded  

>"mute notifications"
