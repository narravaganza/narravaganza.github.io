The Narravaganza CollectionofArt That's Fine

The Narravaganza Collection of Art That's Fine brings together sixteen works in which the proper name becomes a material proposition. Names associated with particular artistic languages acquire bodies, objects and settings, extending their meanings into the visible world.

Presented together, the works consider how recognition takes place across language and image. They reward a knowledge of art history while reserving the right to make other uses of it. Viewers are invited to follow these transfers of meaning, including those for which the artist can no longer reasonably be held responsible.
