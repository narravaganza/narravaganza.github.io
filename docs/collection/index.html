Collection About

The Narravaganza collection brings together investigations into subjects including language, intelligence, cycling, spiritual authority, and the correct treatment of grapes. Methods vary. Some enquiries proceed through dialogue; others require a bicycle, an ice cream shop, or an entire museum.

The findings are available below as stories, sketches, performance texts, and illustrated works.
